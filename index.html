C.O.R.E.

v0.1 · Prototype

Candidate Origin Recognition Engine

Every origin story starts with a day job. Say or type what you already do — C.O.R.E. finds the AI-adjacent version of you that is hiring this quarter, then puts you in touch with someone who is already there.

Hackathon build, and honest about it: the matching engine, the roadmap and every outbound link are real and run entirely in your browser — but the ally profiles are illustrative, and the beacon only saves to this device. Each screen says which parts are which.

> Power Signature Scan

No resume. No account. No forms. Just describe what you do all day.

> People Who Already Made The Jump

Hard-won knowledge moving sideways through a community, instead of getting rediscovered alone at 11pm.

These profiles are illustrative, built from documented transition patterns rather than real individuals — so Find on LinkedIn searches for real people currently in that role, which is more useful anyway. Replace the ALLIES array in assets/js/data.js with your own roster to make them individually linkable.

> Distress Beacon

Stuck on something? Send up a flare. Anyone can answer.

No signals yet

Signals are stored in this browser only — nothing is uploaded, and nobody else can see them yet. Point loadBeacon() and saveBeacon() in assets/js/community.js at a Supabase or Firebase free-tier table to make the beacon genuinely multi-user.

For the people not in the room

Build my four-week plan

Answer four questions. Get a week-by-week plan that fits the time you actually have, with a real free link on every single task. No jargon, no login, no laptop needed.
